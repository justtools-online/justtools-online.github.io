FAQ

About JustTools

What is JustTools?

JustTools is a free collection of online tools for images, text and everyday tasks. Every tool runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to create.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. JustTools processes files and text locally in your browser, so your data is not uploaded, stored or shared. If a future tool ever needs the internet to do its job — such as looking up a website — its page will say so clearly.

Is JustTools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no usage limits, watermarks or sign-up. The site is funded by advertising; ads never lock a feature, and the files and text you work with are never shared with advertisers.

Do the tools work on my phone?

Yes. Every tool is designed for touch screens and works in any modern browser on Android, iPhone and iPad, as well as on Windows, Mac and Linux computers.

Can I request a new tool?

Please do. Send your idea through the contact page — requests help decide which tools are built next.
