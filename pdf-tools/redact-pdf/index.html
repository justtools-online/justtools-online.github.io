Redact PDF

Hide personal details in a PDF permanently. Draw boxes or let the tool find emails, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN and card numbers — the text under each box is removed, not just covered, and nothing is uploaded.

Runs in your browser Free · no sign-up Works on any device

At a glance

  • True redaction: every page with a box is saved as an image with the box burned in, so the text, images and numbers underneath are removed from the file, not hidden under a shape.
  • After saving, the file is re-opened and checked: redacted pages must contain no hidden text.
  • Find & mark detects email addresses, phone numbers, Aadhaar numbers (checked with the Verhoeff checksum), PAN numbers, card numbers (Luhn-checked) and any names or words you type.
  • Draw boxes by hand with a mouse or finger — for signatures, photos, stamps and scanned documents.
  • Document properties (title, author, subject, creator) are removed by default, as they can contain names too.
  • Pages without boxes are copied unchanged, so their text stays selectable.
  • Runs entirely in your browser: the PDF is never uploaded.

Step by step

How to redact a PDF

  1. 1

    Open the PDF

    Drop a PDF onto the box or click to choose one. Enter its password if it has one.

  2. 2

    Mark what to hide

    Use Find & mark for emails, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, card numbers or your own words, and drag on the page to draw extra boxes.

  3. 3

    Check every page

    Go through the pages with the arrows or the thumbnails. Click × on a box to remove it.

  4. 4

    Redact and download

    Click Redact & download. The redacted copy downloads and is verified to contain no hidden text on the redacted pages.

Why a black box isn’t enough

A PDF keeps its text separately from what you see. Drawing a black rectangle over a number — in a PDF editor, or by highlighting it black — leaves the number in the file, and anyone can copy it out or remove the rectangle. Court filings and government reports have leaked this way.

This tool redraws every page that has a box as an image, with the boxes painted in. The original text, images and drawings on that page are not copied into the new file, so there is nothing underneath to recover. The trade-off is that text on redacted pages can no longer be selected or searched; pages without boxes are untouched.

What to redact before sharing a document

  • Aadhaar copies: the first 8 digits (UIDAI’s “masked Aadhaar” shows only the last 4), plus the QR code and photo if they aren’t needed.
  • Bank statements: account numbers, balances and transactions that aren’t relevant to the request.
  • Salary slips and ITRs: PAN, UAN, bank details and home address.
  • Medical reports: patient ID, phone number and address when sharing for a second opinion.
  • Also check headers, footers and stamps — and after redacting, add a “for KYC only” watermark if you like.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the redaction permanent?

Yes. Pages with boxes are rebuilt as images with the boxes burned in, so the covered text is not in the file at all. The tool then re-opens the file to confirm no text remains on those pages.

Can I redact an Aadhaar number automatically?

Yes. Tick Aadhaar numbers and click Find and mark. Only numbers with a valid Aadhaar checksum are marked, so dates and other 12-digit numbers aren’t caught by mistake. You can adjust the boxes afterwards.

Why can’t I select text on redacted pages any more?

Because those pages are now images — that is what guarantees the hidden text is gone. Pages you didn’t redact keep their selectable text.

Does Find & mark work on scanned PDFs?

No — scans are pictures, with no text to search. Draw boxes over the areas to hide instead; the result is just as secure.

Is it safe to redact confidential documents here?

Yes. The PDF is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded, so it stays on your device.

Will the redacted PDF be larger?

Redacted pages are stored as images, which is usually larger than text. Choose Smaller quality, or compress the PDF afterwards.
