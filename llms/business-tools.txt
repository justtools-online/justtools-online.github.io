### Guide

#### How the deductions are calculated

| Deduction | Rule used |
| --- | --- |
| Provident fund (PF) | 12% of Basic + DA; with the wage ceiling, on at most ₹15,000 (₹1,800 a month) |
| Employer PF | Another 12%: 8.33% to the pension scheme (on up to ₹15,000) and the rest to PF |

#### Loss of pay

When an employee is paid for fewer days than the month has, each earning is reduced in proportion: **earned = monthly amount × paid days ÷ days in the month**. For example, ₹45,000 Basic with 27 of 30 days paid gives ₹40,500. PF and ESI are then worked out on the reduced pay.

When the employee’s gross pay is ₹21,000 a month or less. The employee pays 0.75% and the employer 3.25%, both rounded up to the next rupee.

### Guide

#### What a Luxembourg invoice must show

Article 63 §8 of the VAT law lists the details; the company law of 10 August 1915 adds the identification of companies. This tool prints all of them:

- The **date of issue** and a **unique sequential number**, which may follow one or more series.
- Your **VAT number**, and the **customer’s VAT number** when they account for the VAT (reverse charge) or receive an intra-Community supply of goods.
- The **full name and address** of both parties.
- The **quantity and nature** of the goods or the **extent and nature** of the services, and the **date of supply** if it differs from the invoice date.
- The **taxable amount for each rate** or exemption, the **unit price excluding VAT**, discounts, the **rate** and the **VAT due**.
- For an exemption, the **legal reference**; for reverse charge, **“Autoliquidation”**; with cash accounting, **“Comptabilité de caisse”**.
- For a company: its name with the **legal form** (SA, SARL…), the **registered office** and **“R.C.S. Luxembourg”** with its registration number.

#### Luxembourg VAT rates

Which rate applies depends on the annexes of the VAT law and its regulations — check with the AED (Administration de l’enregistrement, des domaines et de la TVA) if you are not sure for a given product.

| Rate | Name | Typical uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 17 % | Normal rate | Most goods and services |
| 14 % | Intermediate rate | Wines up to 13 % alcohol, mineral fuels and heating oil, washing and cleaning products, advertising print, custody of securities (annex C) |
| 8 % | Reduced rate | Gas, electricity, district heating, firewood, hairdressing, repairs of shoes, clothes and household appliances, bicycles (annex A) |
| 3 % | Super-reduced rate | Food (not alcoholic drinks), books and newspapers, children’s clothes, medicines, restaurant meals (not alcohol), hotels, passenger transport, entry to culture and sport (annex B) |

#### Small businesses: the VAT franchise

Businesses established in Luxembourg whose annual turnover in Luxembourg does not exceed **€50,000** (the threshold since 1 January 2025) can use the franchise: they charge no VAT and cannot deduct it. If turnover passes €50,000 the exemption continues until the end of the year as long as it stays within €55,000; above that it ends the next day (AED SME scheme FAQ).

Franchise invoices may not show VAT and must carry the statement “TVA non applicable – Article 57bis de la loi modifiée du 12 février 1979”. Since 2025 they may also be simplified invoices.

#### E-invoicing in Luxembourg

Invoices to Luxembourg **public bodies** must be electronic, in the European standard EN 16931 sent over Peppol — for all suppliers since 18 March 2023. The **e-invoice (UBL)** button makes such a file; send it through a Peppol access point or the State’s e-invoicing service.

For **business-to-business** invoices, a government bill (n° 8815, filed in July 2026) proposes compulsory e-invoicing from 2028 for receiving and from 2028–2029 for issuing. It is still a draft; until it becomes law, PDF invoices remain fine between businesses.

### FAQ

**Do I have to write my invoice in French?**
No. Invoices can be in any language, but the AED may ask for a French or German translation of invoices in another language (art. 63 §12). French, German and English — or bilingual — are built in.

**When must an invoice be issued?**
At the latest on the 15th day of the month after the month of the supply, and for an advance payment at the latest when you receive it (art. 63 §5). The checklist warns you when the invoice date is later.

**What do I write for a customer in another EU country?**
For services to a business, no Luxembourg VAT is charged and the invoice says “Autoliquidation” (reverse charge), with the customer’s VAT number. For goods shipped to a VAT-registered business in another EU country, the supply is exempt (art. 43 §1 d) and the invoice says so. Choose the matching regime and the wording is added.

**I am a freelancer under the franchise. What goes on my invoice?**
No VAT, and the statement “TVA non applicable – Article 57bis de la loi modifiée du 12 février 1979”. Switch on “I use the VAT franchise” and the tool does the rest.

**Is an RCS number required on invoices?**
For commercial companies, yes: the 1915 company law requires the name, legal form, registered office and “R.C.S. Luxembourg” followed by the registration number on invoices and letters.

**What is a simplified invoice?**
An invoice with fewer details, allowed when the total including VAT is no more than €100, for documents that amend an earlier invoice, and for businesses under the franchise (art. 63 §10–11). This tool always produces a full invoice, which is valid in every case.

**Does the SEPA QR code work with my bank?**
It follows the European Payments Council standard (EPC069-12, also called GiroCode). Many banking apps in the euro area can read it; if a customer’s app cannot, the IBAN and reference are printed beside it.

**Is my data uploaded?**
No. The invoice is built in your browser. Your business details, logo and customers are saved on this device only, so they are there next time.

Sign at your natural speed — the pen effect depends on it. On a phone, turn the screen sideways for a wider pad. For photo uploads, sign with a dark pen on plain white paper, photograph it straight on in daylight, and raise the sensitivity if grey shadows remain. Many government forms ask for a signature of a given size, such as 140 × 60 pixels and under 20 KB; use the Image Resizer and Image Compressor to match.

### FAQ

**Are rent receipts made online valid for HRA?**
Yes. Employers need the details — names, address, amount, period, the landlord’s PAN where required — and the landlord’s signature. A printed receipt signed by the landlord is accepted the same as a handwritten one.

**When is the landlord’s PAN required?**
When the rent you pay is more than ₹1,00,000 in the year (about ₹8,334 a month). If the landlord has no PAN, they give you a signed declaration saying so.

**Do rent receipts need a revenue stamp?**
Only receipts for cash payments above ₹5,000 need a ₹1 revenue stamp signed across by the landlord. Rent paid by UPI, bank transfer or cheque doesn’t.

**Monthly or quarterly receipts?**
Both are accepted by most employers. Quarterly receipts mean four signatures a year instead of twelve.

**Can I pay rent to my parents and claim HRA?**
Yes, if the house is owned by them, you really pay the rent (preferably by bank transfer), and they show it as income in their tax return.

Only in this browser, so the form is filled in next time. Nothing is uploaded.
