## GST Invoice Generator

URL: https://justtools.online/business-tools/gst-invoice-generator/
Category: Business Tools
Processing: Runs entirely in the browser; user data is not uploaded.
Last updated: 2026-10-07

Make GST tax invoices in your browser: CGST, SGST or IGST worked out, GSTIN check, HSN summary and a UPI QR code. Download a PDF — free, no sign-up.

### At a glance

- Makes **tax invoices**, **bills of supply**, **proforma invoices** and **quotations** as a ready-to-send A4 PDF with real, searchable text.
- Charges **CGST + SGST** when the place of supply is in your state and **IGST** when it isn’t — per item, rounded to the paisa, with an **HSN/SAC-wise tax summary**.
- **Checks GSTINs** (format, state code and check digit) and fills in the state and PAN from them.
- Adds a **UPI QR code** for the exact amount, your bank details, logo, signature, the amount in words and an optional **original, duplicate and triplicate** set.
- Handles **discounts**, **GST-inclusive prices**, round-off and numbering (INV-007 → INV-008). Long item lists continue onto more pages.
- Works **offline** once loaded: your business details, customers and the last invoice are saved only in this browser, with a backup file you can keep.

### Features

- **GST worked out**: Choose 0, 5, 18% or another GST rate for each item; the right split of CGST, SGST or IGST is calculated for you.
- **GSTIN check**: Typos in a GSTIN are caught by its check digit before the invoice goes out.
- **Get paid faster**: A UPI QR code for the invoice amount lets customers pay from any UPI app.
- **Looks professional**: Your logo, colours and signature on a clean layout with the totals in words.
- **Saved customers**: Save regular customers once and pick them for the next bill.
- **Private and offline**: No account, no server: the PDF is made on your device and nothing is uploaded.

### How to make a GST invoice

1. **Enter your business details** — Add your name, address, GSTIN, bank and UPI ID. They are saved for your next invoice.
2. **Add the customer** — Type the buyer’s details and GSTIN (if registered), or pick a saved customer. The place of supply sets CGST/SGST or IGST.
3. **Add items** — Enter each item with its HSN/SAC code, quantity, rate, discount and GST rate. Totals update as you type.
4. **Download the PDF** — Check the preview and press **Download PDF**. Use **Next invoice** to start the next one with the following number.

### Guide

#### What a GST tax invoice must show

- Your name, address and **GSTIN**, and a **unique invoice number** (up to 16 characters) and date.
- The buyer’s name and address, and their GSTIN if they are registered.
- The **place of supply** (state name and code), for supplies between states.
- For each item: description, **HSN or SAC code**, quantity and unit, value, discount and the **taxable value**.
- The GST rate and amount — **CGST and SGST**, or **IGST** — and the invoice total.
- Whether tax is payable on **reverse charge**, and the signature of the supplier or an authorised person.

#### CGST, SGST or IGST?

| Supply | Tax charged | Example at 18% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Within the same state (intra-state) | CGST + SGST, half each | 9% CGST + 9% SGST |
| Within a union territory without a legislature | CGST + UTGST, half each | 9% CGST + 9% UTGST |
| To another state (inter-state) | IGST | 18% IGST |

### FAQ

**Is this GST invoice generator free?**
Yes — free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on invoices.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Once the page has loaded, invoices are made entirely in your browser, so you can keep working without internet. Your details are saved on this device only.

**When is IGST charged instead of CGST and SGST?**
When the place of supply is in a different state from yours. Within your state, the GST rate is split equally into CGST and SGST. The invoice switches automatically when you choose the customer’s state.

**What is a bill of supply?**
An invoice without GST, issued by composition dealers or for exempt goods and services. Choose **Bill of Supply** and no tax is charged or shown.

**Can I add a UPI QR code to the invoice?**
Yes. Enter your UPI ID under Payment; the PDF gets a QR code for the exact invoice amount that any UPI app can scan.

**Is my data safe?**
Nothing is uploaded. Your business details, customers and invoices stay in this browser — download a backup file to move them to another device.

**Does it make e-invoices with an IRN?**
No. E-invoicing (IRN and QR from the government portal) is required only above a turnover limit and needs the IRP portal. This tool makes regular GST invoices.

## Salary Slip Generator

URL: https://justtools.online/business-tools/salary-slip-generator/
Category: Business Tools
Processing: Runs entirely in the browser; user data is not uploaded.
Last updated: 2026-10-07

Make monthly salary slips as PDF: earnings, PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS worked out, loss of pay and net pay in words. Free, private, no sign-up.

### At a glance

- Makes a one-page **A4 payslip PDF** with your company logo, employee details, earnings, deductions and **net pay in figures and words**.
- Works out **employee PF** (12% of Basic + DA, with the optional ₹15,000 wage ceiling) and **ESI** (0.75% while the monthly gross is ₹21,000 or less, rounded up).
- Adds **professional tax**, **TDS** and any other deductions such as loans or advances.
- Handles **loss of pay**: earnings are paid for the days worked, with monthly and earned amounts shown side by side.
- Can show the **employer’s PF and ESI** and the month’s cost to company, and split a monthly gross into Basic, HRA and special allowance.
- Everything is saved only in this browser; press **Next month** to make the following payslip.

### Features

- **Statutory deductions**: PF and ESI follow the current rates and limits, so you don’t calculate them by hand.
- **Loss of pay**: Enter the paid days; every earning is pro-rated automatically.
- **Your branding**: Company logo, address and colours on a clean, printable layout.
- **Month to month**: Save the structure once and create next month’s payslip in a click.
- **Net pay in words**: Written the Indian way — lakh and crore, rupees and paise.
- **Private**: Salary details never leave your device; nothing is uploaded or stored online.

### How to make a salary slip

1. **Enter company and employee** — Add the company name and logo, then the employee’s name, ID, designation, PAN, UAN and bank.
2. **Set the salary** — Enter each earning for a full month — or type the monthly gross and press **Split**.
3. **Check deductions** — PF and ESI are worked out; add professional tax, TDS and any other deductions, and the paid days.
4. **Download** — Press **Download PDF**. Next month, press **Next month** and change only what’s different.

### Guide

#### How the deductions are calculated

| Deduction | Rule used |
| --- | --- |
| Provident fund (PF) | 12% of Basic + DA; with the wage ceiling, on at most ₹15,000 (₹1,800 a month) |
| Employer PF | Another 12%: 8.33% to the pension scheme (on up to ₹15,000) and the rest to PF |
| ESI (employee) | 0.75% of gross pay when the monthly gross is ₹21,000 or less, rounded up to the rupee |
| ESI (employer) | 3.25% of gross pay on the same condition |
| Professional tax | Set by each state, up to ₹2,500 a year — enter your state’s amount |
| TDS | Income tax deducted at source, as worked out for the employee’s regime |

#### Loss of pay

When an employee is paid for fewer days than the month has, each earning is reduced in proportion: **earned = monthly amount × paid days ÷ days in the month**. For example, ₹45,000 Basic with 27 of 30 days paid gives ₹40,500. PF and ESI are then worked out on the reduced pay.

### FAQ

**Is this salary slip generator free?**
Yes — free, without sign-up or watermark, for as many employees and months as you like.

**How is PF calculated on a payslip?**
The employee contributes 12% of Basic + DA. Most employers apply it on wages of at most ₹15,000, which caps it at ₹1,800 a month; switch the ceiling off to use the full Basic.

**When is ESI deducted?**
When the employee’s gross pay is ₹21,000 a month or less. The employee pays 0.75% and the employer 3.25%, both rounded up to the next rupee.

**Is a computer-generated payslip valid?**
Yes. Payslips from payroll software are routinely accepted without a signature; the PDF says it is computer-generated. Some banks or landlords may ask for a stamped copy or a salary certificate.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only in this browser. Download a backup file to keep the details or move them to another computer.
