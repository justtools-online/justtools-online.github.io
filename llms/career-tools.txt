### Guide

#### Which sections to include

| Section | Freshers | Experienced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summary | 2–3 lines on your skills and the role you want | 2–4 lines on your experience and biggest strengths |
| Experience | Internships, part-time work, freelancing | Most recent first, 3–5 achievement bullets each |
| Projects | College and personal projects — put them high up | Optional; side projects that show relevant skills |
| Education | Near the top, with CGPA or percentage | Near the end; the score can be left out |
| Skills | Tools, languages and software you can really use | Grouped: languages, tools, methods |
| Certifications | Online courses and certificates | Professional certifications |
| Personal details & declaration | Often asked for in campus and government jobs | Usually left out for private-sector and overseas jobs |

#### How to write bullet points that get shortlisted

- Start with an action verb — **Led, Built, Reduced, Increased, Launched** — not “Responsible for”.
- Show the result with a number: “Cut report time from 3 days to 4 hours”, “Handled 120 customer calls a day”, “Saved ₹8 lakh a year”.
- Keep each bullet to one or two lines, and 3–5 bullets per job.
- Use the words from the job description for skills you really have — ATS software ranks resumes by matching them.
- Keep the resume to one page with under about five years of experience, and two pages at most.

**Should I add a photo, date of birth and declaration?**
In India they are common for government, PSU and campus applications. Leave them out for private-sector, MNC and overseas jobs, where recruiters prefer not to see them.

**What is the best resume format for freshers?**
One page: contact details, a two-line summary, education with CGPA, projects and internships with results, skills and certifications. The example resume shows the structure — replace it with your details.

**Can I write my resume in Hindi?**
Hindi text prints correctly in the PDF, but most companies and ATS software expect an English resume, and some systems read Hindi text imperfectly.

3. **Look at the ATS view** — See the text and details the ATS extracts. Jumbled lines mean the layout needs simplifying.
4. **Match a job** — Paste the job description to find missing skills and keywords, then add the ones you really have.

### Guide

#### What ATS software needs from a resume

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Real, readable text | Scanned or image-only resumes come through empty. |
| A simple layout | Columns, tables and text boxes can mix up or hide text. |
| Standard headings | Experience, Education and Skills tell the ATS where each detail belongs. |
| Contact details in the page | Some ATS ignore Word headers and footers. |
| Dates on every role | They are used to work out your years of experience. |
| Keywords from the job | Recruiters search and rank candidates by skills and job titles. |

#### ATS myths

- **“An ATS rejects most resumes automatically.”** Most systems only reject through screening questions you answer while applying, such as location or notice period. Otherwise recruiters decide — but they find candidates by searching what the ATS extracted, so a resume it reads badly rarely comes up.
- **“You need a special ATS template.”** A clean, single-column PDF or Word file with standard headings works with every major ATS. Elaborate designs are what cause problems.
- **“Add as many keywords as you can.”** A person reads the resume after the search. Add only skills you really have, and show them in results.
- **“PDFs aren’t ATS-friendly.”** Modern ATS read text-based PDFs well. Scanned PDFs and image-heavy designs are the problem — this checker shows which kind yours is.

### FAQ

**What is an ATS score?**
There is no single official score — every ATS (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Naukri RMS and others) works a little differently. This score measures what they all depend on: whether the text can be read, whether your details and sections are recognised, and how clearly your achievements are written.

**Is my resume uploaded anywhere?**
No. The file is opened and checked by your browser; nothing is sent to a server, and it is gone when you close the page.

**What is a good ATS score?**
75 or more means software reads your resume well and the content is solid; 90 or more is excellent. More important than the number: fix every critical problem and warning.

**Why is my job match low?**
The match compares your resume with the skills and keywords in that job ad. Add the ones you really have — in your skills and in bullets that show them in use — with the same spelling as the job. Don’t add skills you don’t have.

**Are two-column resumes bad for ATS?**
Modern systems often cope, but many read across the page and mix the columns together. If the ATS view shows jumbled lines, switch to one column — the free [Resume Builder](https://justtools.online/career-tools/resume-builder/) makes one.

**Should I send a PDF or a Word file?**
Either works. Send what the job asks for; otherwise a text-based PDF keeps your layout exactly. Use .docx when a portal or recruiter asks for Word, and avoid scanned PDFs, images and old .doc files.
