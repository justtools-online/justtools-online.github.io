Image Watermark

Protect your photos with a text or logo watermark — one corner mark or a repeating pattern — see it live, then watermark a whole batch and download them as a ZIP.

Runs in your browser Free · no sign-up Works on any device

At a glance

  • Adds a text watermark (any language, including Hindi, and emoji) or your logo (PNG with transparency, JPG, WebP or SVG) to photos.
  • Place it once in any of nine positions, or repeat it across the photo at any angle — hardest to crop or remove.
  • Size is relative to each photo, so a batch of large and small images gets a matching watermark.
  • An optional outline keeps white or dark text readable on busy backgrounds.
  • Watermark many photos at once and download them as a ZIP, in their original format (JPG, PNG or WebP; HEIC becomes JPG) at full size.
  • Runs in your browser — photos and logos are never uploaded. Camera data (EXIF, GPS) is not copied to the watermarked files.

Protecting photos you post online

A small corner watermark tells people who took the photo and is easy to live with — but it can be cropped out. If your images are being copied, use a larger, semi-transparent watermark across the subject, or repeat it across the whole photo.

For documents such as Aadhaar or PAN copies, add the purpose and date (“For KYC only · 30 Sep 2026”) across the image so a copy can’t be reused elsewhere. For PDFs, use PDF Watermark.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I watermark many photos at once?

Yes. Add as many photos as you like; the same watermark is applied to all of them, scaled to each photo’s size, and you can download them together as a ZIP.

Will my photos lose quality?

They keep their full size. JPG and WebP photos are saved at high quality (92), and PNG stays lossless.

Can I use my logo as a watermark?

Yes. Choose Logo / image and upload a PNG with a transparent background (or JPG, WebP or SVG). Set its width and opacity.

Can a watermark be removed?

A small corner mark can be cropped or edited out. A large, rotated watermark repeated across the photo is much harder to remove without ruining the image.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos and your logo are processed in your browser on your own device.

Can I write the watermark in Hindi?

Yes. The text is drawn by your browser, so Hindi, other Indian languages and emoji all work.
