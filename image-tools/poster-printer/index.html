Poster Printer

Turn a photo, drawing or PDF page into a poster made of ordinary sheets: choose the size, see exactly how the sheets fall, and print one PDF with overlap, cut marks and an assembly guide.

Runs in your browser Free · no sign-up Works on any device

At a glance

  • Splits a picture or a PDF page across A4, US Letter, A3, Legal, Tabloid or A5 sheets, up to 400 of them, as one PDF for any home printer.
  • Set the size in sheets (for example 3 across) or in cm or inches, or fit it to A0–A3 or an 18 × 24, 24 × 36 or 27 × 40 inch poster. On Auto, the sheets are turned whichever way needs fewer of them.
  • Neighbouring sheets can overlap by up to 30 mm for gluing, with a dashed line to line them up. Each sheet gets cut marks and a name such as B3, with its neighbours listed.
  • An optional first page holds the assembly guide: a map of the sheets, the steps and a 10 cm line to check the printer did not shrink the page. The PDF also asks PDF readers to print at actual size.
  • Shows print quality as you resize: the picture’s pixels per inch and the distance from which single pixels can no longer be seen. A PDF page stays vector, sharp at any size.
  • A dots style turns the picture into a halftone of black, coloured or single-colour dots, the look the Rasterbator made popular.
  • JPGs and PNGs go into the PDF unchanged, and the picture is stored once however many sheets there are, so the file stays small. Nothing is uploaded.

Step by step

How to print a poster on A4 or Letter pages

  1. 1

    Add your picture

    Drop a JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC photo, paste one, or choose a PDF and the page to print.

  2. 2

    Choose the size

    Under Poster size, pick how many Sheets across or down, or switch to Size for a width, height or standard poster size.

  3. 3

    Set the paper

    Choose the sheet size, how the sheets are turned, the printer margin and the overlap for gluing. Check the preview and the print quality.

  4. 4

    Make the PDF

    Press Make poster PDF, look through the pages, then Download PDF.

  5. 5

    Print and assemble

    Print at Actual size / 100 %, check the 10 cm line on the guide page, trim the margins and glue the sheets in the order of their names.

How big can you print a photo?

Print quality depends on how many of the picture’s pixels land on each inch of paper (ppi). Close up, 150 ppi or more looks sharp. A poster is usually seen from further away, where fewer pixels per inch are enough: the table gives the distance at which single pixels become too small to see, taking sharp eyesight as one minute of arc.

Tips for a neat poster

  • Print a single test sheet first and check that the 10 cm line really measures 10 cm.
  • Turn off borderless printing and any “fit”, “shrink” or “scale to page” option.
  • Heavier paper (100–120 g/m²) or matte photo paper wrinkles less under glue.
  • Trim with a metal ruler and a craft knife; scissors leave wavy edges that show at the joins.
  • Use a glue stick or double-sided tape in the overlap strips; wet glue makes paper ripple.
  • Join each row first, then join the rows – it is easier to keep long edges straight.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I print one picture on several pages?

Add the picture here, choose how many sheets across it should be (or its final size), then press Make poster PDF. The PDF has one page per sheet; print them all at actual size and join them in the order of their names – A1, A2, then B1, B2 and so on.

Why don’t my printed sheets line up?

Almost always because the print dialog scaled the pages: “Fit to page” or “Shrink to printable area” makes each sheet slightly smaller. Choose Actual size or 100 % and check the 10 cm line on the guide page. If it is still off, set the printer margin in the tool to at least what your printer needs.

What printer margin should I use?

Most inkjet and laser printers need 3–6 mm on each side. The default of 10 mm is safe for almost every printer and leaves room for the sheet names. Your printer’s manual or its “borderless” settings tell you the exact minimum.

How many A4 pages make an A1 poster?

An A1 sheet has the area of 8 A4 sheets, but because each page loses its margins and shares an overlap with its neighbours you need more: 15 A4 sheets with 10 mm margins and 10 mm overlap, or 9 with 5 mm margins and no overlap.

Can I print a PDF poster on A4 sheets?

Yes. Choose the PDF and the page; the page is copied into each sheet as vector graphics, so text and lines stay crisp at any size. A password-protected PDF can be opened with its password, which is used only on your device.

What is rasterbation?

It is the name the Rasterbator gave to making huge posters out of halftone dots. Choose the Dots style: each dot is as big as the picture is dark at that spot, in black, in the picture’s own colours, or in one colour of your choice. Seen from a few metres away the dots merge into the picture.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. The picture or PDF is opened and laid out by your browser, and the poster PDF is made on your device. Only your size and paper settings are saved, in this browser.
