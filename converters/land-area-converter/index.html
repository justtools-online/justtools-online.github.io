Why sizes differ — and what to rely on

Bigha, biswa and katha are older than any national standard, and each region kept its own. Many places even have two: a larger pucca bigha used in records and a smaller kaccha bigha used in everyday talk.

Government records are kept in hectares (or acres), and sale deeds are registered in square metres or hectares. For a purchase, loan or mutation, take the area from the record of rights — khatauni, jamabandi, 7/12 extract or patta — and confirm the local unit with the lekhpal, patwari or tehsil office. Then type that size here so the conversion matches.

Formulas

  • Plot area in sq ft = length (ft) × width (ft).
  • Gaj (sq yd) = sq ft ÷ 9.
  • Square metres = sq ft ÷ 10.7639.
  • Acres = sq ft ÷ 43,560. Hectares = sq ft ÷ 1,07,639.
  • Bigha = sq ft ÷ the bigha of your state — for example ÷ 27,000 in Uttar Pradesh or ÷ 14,400 in West Bengal.
  • Rate per bigha = rate per sq ft × sq ft in a bigha.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in 1 bigha?

It depends on the state: 27,000 sq ft in Uttar Pradesh, 27,220 in Bihar, 27,225 in Rajasthan and Haryana, 17,424 in Gujarat, 14,400 in West Bengal and Assam, 12,000 in Madhya Pradesh, 9,070 in Punjab and 8,712 in Himachal Pradesh. Choose your state above to use its value.

How many bigha are in 1 acre?

About 1.61 bigha in Uttar Pradesh, 1.6 in Bihar, Rajasthan and Haryana, 2.5 in Gujarat, 3.025 in West Bengal and Assam, 3.63 in Madhya Pradesh, 4.8 in Punjab and exactly 5 in Himachal Pradesh.

How many bigha are in 1 hectare?

One hectare is 1,07,639 sq ft: about 3.99 bigha in Uttar Pradesh, 3.95 in Bihar and Rajasthan, 6.18 in Gujarat and 7.47 in West Bengal and Assam.

How many square feet are in 1 katha?

A katha is 720 sq ft in West Bengal, about 1,361 sq ft in Bihar, 2,880 sq ft in Assam and 600 sq ft in Madhya Pradesh. In Bihar it changes from district to district, so check your record.

What is a gaj?

A gaj is a square yard: 9 sq ft, or 0.836 sq m. A 100 gaj plot is 900 sq ft (83.61 sq m), and an acre is 4,840 gaj.

How many guntha are in an acre?

40 guntha make an acre. One guntha is 1,089 sq ft — 121 gaj, or 101.17 sq m.

How big is a kanal or a marla?

A kanal is 5,445 sq ft (605 gaj) and a marla is 272.25 sq ft (30.25 gaj). 20 marla make a kanal and 8 kanal make an acre (killa).

My area uses a different bigha. Can I change it?

Yes. Under Unit sizes, type the size in square feet from your land record. Biswa, katha and other sub-units follow it automatically, and your value is remembered on your device.

Is anything I enter uploaded?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Only your chosen state, units and corrected sizes are saved, in your browser’s local storage.
